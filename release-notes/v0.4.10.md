# FreeBank app v0.4.10 "Copy words, safer"

Copying your recovery words is safer, above all on a Mac.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **On a Mac, copied words stay on this Mac.** "Copy words" now writes them for this Mac only, so Universal Clipboard
  doesn't offer them to your iPhone or other Apple devices, and marks them for clipboard managers to skip.
- **The clipboard is cleared in more cases:** after 60 seconds as before, and now also when FreeBank quits, is told to
  stop (a SIGTERM, SIGINT or SIGHUP), or crashes in its own code.
- **Pasting your words in clears the clipboard.** When you paste all your words into the check step or into Restore,
  FreeBank takes them and then clears the clipboard (on Linux also the middle-click selection), and says whether it
  could make sure they are gone.
- **The check step tells FreeBank's own copy from the clipboard itself**, not from whether you left the window: a copy
  FreeBank is about to clear is no backup, so it is refused.
- **A numbered list must line up.** Restoring from a numbered list works when every word has its number and they run
  1 to 24; FreeBank shows the words in that order before restoring. A list whose numbers don't line up is refused
  instead of guessed at.
- **No core dump of FreeBank's own process with your words in it** (Linux).
- **eCash transaction IDs.** After a send in the eCash tab you see the whole transaction ID, with Copy. Each payment
  in the list has Details: its transaction ID, address and time.

The changes to copying words come from the Truthcoin app, which took FreeBank's copy words and had it reviewed four
times.

## Honest limits

- A copy you make in another app (Notes, a password manager) has none of these protections.
- FreeBank can't clear what a clipboard history has already kept; it says so.
- On a Wayland desktop FreeBank can't check that the clipboard is clear, so it says that instead.
- A `kill -9`, or a crash outside FreeBank's own code, skips the clear.
- The screens run in WebKit's own process, which Linux can still dump. If it crashes while your words are on screen,
  Ubuntu's crash reporter can keep a copy of its memory in `/var/crash`: don't send that report, and delete it.
