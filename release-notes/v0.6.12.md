# FreeBank app v0.6.12 "ways to use FreeBank"

Not sure what to do with FreeBank? **Ways to use FreeBank** asks what you want to do and takes you through it, step by
step. This version also fixes Claim the bond, which never came on for an unpaid bill.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Ways to use FreeBank,** on Home, at the top of Credit, and in Settings › About › Help. Eight goals, from giving a
  customer time to pay to running a house. Each guide says who lends, who carries the risk, what it costs and how much
  the app does today, then gives the steps, with a button to each screen. The bill guides turn days into blocks and a
  price into sECX. It is general information, the same for everyone.
- **Tell us what's missing.** Each guide lists what FreeBank can't do yet, and **Send as a suggestion** fills in the
  report form for you to read and send.
- **Claim the bond works.** A bill you hold that wasn't paid in time now says "Past due", and Claim the bond takes the
  bond. The button used to wait for a status the node sets only after the claim.

## Honest limits

- Selling a bill to a house, borrowing from one and term deposits are still from the command line; the guides say so.
- On easynet, notes, bills, pools and deposits pause until FreeBank-easynet block 600 (FreeBank v1.0.2).
- Not yet run on a Mac.
