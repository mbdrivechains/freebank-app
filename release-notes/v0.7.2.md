On easynet, your phone can now send, redeem and demand a house's notes in grams, and a house's owners can pledge gold
and run its till from the app. It shows what they're worth at the
gold price, and counts that against its daily limit, so larger amounts wait for you on the desktop as before.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Notes from the phone, in grams.** On easynet, Notes on the phone offer **Send**, **Redeem** and **Demand** again,
  with the amount in grams. The phone shows roughly what the notes are worth in sECX at the gold price, and that worth,
  with the fee, counts against the phone's daily limit. When the desktop is asked to confirm, it says both: "send
  1.5 g of house #1's notes (about 0.0015 sECX)". With no gold price on FreeBank, moving notes from the phone waits.

- **Gold for your house.** House tools (Credit › Houses) gain **Gold for your house**: for a house your node holds the
  keys of, pledge gold tokens into its owners' capital as one of its partners, or move gold into or out of its till.
  The card shows what each vault holds. "Approve sends on my phone" counts the gold that leaves your wallet at the
  gold price.

## Honest limits

- A hosted wallet's phone (a shop at a members-only house) still moves notes from the desktop on gram-notes chains.
- Tokens stay off on the phone while easynet's house mints are off.
- House note pools are closed on gram-notes chains, so the Pools tab shows only gold-token pools.
- Netting between houses is from the command line for now on gram-notes chains, and the money changer stays off there
  until its quotes are in grams.
- **Ways to use FreeBank** still gives amounts in sECX; on easynet it says so.
- Mainnet stays on FreeBank v1.0.2 until a later release.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
