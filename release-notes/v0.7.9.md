At a failed house, Claim now says in plain words when payouts open and what to do next, and it never spends anything
you didn't ask for. A bill handed to you is yours once it's in a block, and the app now says to wait for that before
you pay for it or hand over goods. Nothing changes on mainnet.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Claims at a failed house (FreeBank v1.2.8 and later).** A failed house now pays out only once every bill it holds
  is repaid or retired, or about 3 months after its collection window ends at the latest. The note card says so, with
  that block. If a bill is due, Claim says so and offers **Collect bill #…** first: its own transaction with its own
  small fee, which "Approve sends on my phone" checks like any other payment. Then claim after the next block. If
  payouts haven't opened yet, Claim lists the bills still out and when each falls due. Nothing is sent until you
  press the button for it.

- **Bills handed on.** Bills › Receive a bill and the "goods now, paid later" guide say that a bill handed to you shows
  once its block is in, usually within ten minutes, and to pay for it or hand over goods only then: until then it can
  still be taken back. The receipt for handing a bill on says the same.

- **Redeeming notes that just arrived.** Since FreeBank v1.2.6, a redeem spends only notes already in a block. When a
  redeem can't be made up from those, the message now says that notes that reached you in the last few minutes can be
  redeemed once their block is in.

## Honest limits

- No house has failed on easynet, so the failed-house screens haven't met a failed house on a live chain. They were
  checked against the node's exact answers in tests, and a FreeBank v1.2.8 node takes the claim's new setting. On
  Linux on 28 November, against FreeBank v1.2.8 on easynet, the app started and synced, and the bill wording showed
  in Receive a bill and in the guide.
- This version hasn't run on a Mac yet; v0.7.4 has.
