Your eCash wallet now opens in Sparrow (and Electrum, given an eCash server), to watch it or spend from it, and coins
held by a Sparrow or Electrum wallet's words can be brought into FreeBank's eCash wallet in one payment that can't be
repeated on Bitcoin. The notice now says a house may pay its notes in gold tokens as well as in sECX, so the app shows
it once more for you to agree to.

## What's new

- **Use this wallet in Sparrow or Electrum** (the eCash tab). Your recovery words open other addresses in those
  wallets, so the card gives them the eCash wallet itself: its watch-only key (zpub, or vpub on easynet) with the
  fingerprint and path, Sparrow's descriptor, and the first address to check against. Behind your passphrase, its
  private key for Sparrow's Master Private Key and Electrum's Use a master key; those can't be copied, so type them or
  scan the QR. It says how to set Sparrow up for eCash first (Testnet on easynet; fee rates from your node; no Bitcoin
  explorer or dollar price; your eCash node as its Bitcoin Core server), and to send eCash from FreeBank where you can:
  FreeBank marks each payment so it can't be repeated on Bitcoin, and Sparrow and Electrum don't.

- **Bring in coins from Sparrow or Electrum** (the eCash tab). Type another wallet's recovery words (Sparrow's BIP39
  words, or Electrum's segwit seed) and its passphrase if it has one. FreeBank finds their coins on eCash and moves
  them all into your eCash wallet in one payment, marked so it can't be repeated on Bitcoin: if the words held bitcoin
  before eCash split, only the ECX moves. FreeBank uses the words for the move and doesn't keep them.

- **The notice: paid in grams or gold tokens.** The first-run notice and Settings › Help › About and legal say that a
  house pays its notes in sECX at an on-chain gold price, or in gold tokens, gram for gram. When you redeem, you sign
  for what you are paid. If you make a formal demand, the house can pay it without you, and it chooses: sECX at a
  recent on-chain price, or gold tokens, the only choice when no price is available. With no gold price, a redemption
  in sECX needs an amount you both sign.

## Honest limits

- Tried with Sparrow 2.5.6 on easynet; not yet on eCash's own network. Electrum's segwit seeds are checked against
  Electrum's own test wallet only, and Electrum can't reach eCash until someone runs an eCash Electrum server.
- Bring in reads native segwit wallets only (bc1q…): not legacy, nested segwit or taproot ones, nor Electrum's older or
  two-factor seeds.
- A hosted wallet's phone (a shop at a members-only house) still moves notes from the desktop on gram-notes chains.
- Tokens stay off on the phone while easynet's house mints are off.
- House note pools are closed on gram-notes chains, so the Pools tab shows only gold-token pools.
- Netting between houses is from the command line for now on gram-notes chains, and the money changer stays off there
  until its quotes are in grams.
- **Ways to use FreeBank** still gives amounts in sECX; on easynet it says so.
- Mainnet stays on FreeBank v1.0.2 until a later release.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
