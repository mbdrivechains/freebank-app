# FreeBank app v0.4.5 "The demo, end to end"

The demo now shows where everything happens: play money and loans come from FreeBank's Telegram bot, and the app is
where you keep and use them.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **How the demo works.** In demo mode, Home has a short guide. On the beta, FreeBank's Telegram bot is the
  cashier: it gives the play money, and the houses lend through it. Its Wallet › Send to your app brings the notes to
  this app, and Credit › Notes is where you use them. **Open the bot** opens it, and Got it folds the guide to one line.
- **DEMO across every page.** A light gold DEMO lies across every screen in demo mode, under the dialogs, so play money is never taken for
  the real thing. Clicks pass through it, and QR codes sit above it so a phone can still read them.
- **Demand, not Redeem, in demo mode.** Redeem pays from the house's reserves, so it needs the house's keys on your
  node. Without them, the node burns the notes and pays nothing. A demo wallet runs no house (its play money is far
  short of a house's pledge), so demo mode hides Redeem, on a paired phone too; in a full setup, Redeem and the phone's
  approval now say so. Demand is the holder's way: the house must pay you in full, and
  FreeBank's beta houses usually pay a demand within the hour.
- **Bills in demo mode** says that the beta's houses lend through the bot, with a link to it.
- **The first screen** says that the demo's play money and loans come from FreeBank's Telegram bot, so you'll need
  Telegram.

## Honest limits

- Everything in v0.4.4's limits still applies: demo mode trusts FreeBank's gateway, has no deposits, withdrawals or
  bidding, and runs only while the app is open.
- Borrowing in the demo happens in the Telegram bot, not in the app, and getting play money needs a Telegram account.
- A demand is paid by the house within its demand window (1008 blocks on the beta). FreeBank's own beta houses usually pay
  within the hour, one demand per house each hour; a house someone else runs pays when its owner does.
