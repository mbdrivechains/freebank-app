On easynet, your phone now shows your notes and the houses in grams of gold, as the desktop does. Moving notes
from the phone waits for a later version, so its daily limit stays right, and Tokens are off on the phone while
easynet's house mints are down.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **The phone in grams.** The phone's **Notes** now open without "Let my phone send" turned on. On easynet they show
  grams, and **Houses** show notes out in grams, each
  house's till against its notes, and how far short redemptions are paid when a spread is in force.
- **Notes move from the desktop for now.** On easynet, sending, redeeming or demanding notes from the phone now says
  to do it in FreeBank on your desktop. Before, the phone counted grams as sECX, so its amounts and its daily limit
  were off by the gold price. Sending sECX from the phone works as before, as does approving the desktop's sends on
  your phone.
- **Tokens are off on the phone** while easynet's house mints are off: they come back once the mints count grams.
- **The full notice** (Settings › Help › About and legal, and NOTICE.md) says that blocks can stop: FreeBank has new
  blocks only while someone bids for them on eCash.

## Honest limits

- Moving notes from the phone in grams comes in a later version.
- House note pools are closed on gram-notes chains, so the Pools tab shows only gold-token pools.
- Running a house in grams (pledging gold, the till) and netting between houses are from the command line for now.
  The money changer stays off on gram-notes chains until its quotes are in grams.
- **Ways to use FreeBank** still gives amounts in sECX; on easynet it says so.
- Mainnet stays on FreeBank v1.0.2 until a later release.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
