# FreeBank app v0.2.3

Ready for FreeBank v0.2.18: the node update rebuilds its data by itself, and notes at a suspended house work by the new
rules. Also: rebuild the Linux package yourself and get the same bytes, and credit always on, with no credit gate.

## What's new

- **The update to FreeBank v0.2.18 finishes by itself.** v0.2.18 is a consensus change that stores the node's data in
  a new format, so it must start once with `-reindex`. It refuses the data an older release wrote and says "Restart
  with -reindex". The app now does that once, by itself, and the Node tab says the node is rebuilding its data until
  it has finished. It rebuilds from the blocks already on your computer (about a minute on the beta chain); your
  wallet stays as it is, though its balance may read low until the rebuild is done. If that start fails too, the
  Node tab shows why and doesn't keep trying. The same happens whenever the node asks for `-reindex`, for example
  after an unclean stop.
- **Why the node stopped,** from that run only: an older run's message no longer shows, and a node the system
  refuses to run now says why (for example a Mac too old for it), from the node's own output.
- **Notes at a suspended house** (FreeBank v0.2.18: suspension has no end date, and demands earn 10% a year):
  - While a house is suspended, only notes under demand can be redeemed, with their interest. Redeem offers exactly
    that amount.
  - Demand says what happens: at a suspended house your notes join its payout queue and earn 10% a year from that
    day; at an open house it is a formal demand the house must pay within the demand window. Either way the notes
    can't be sent while they wait.
  - Demanded notes say which of the two they are (or, at a failed house, that holders are paid from what it has
    left).
  - Redeem and Demand start from the whole amount. Both take notes held at one address, summing exactly to the
    amount; when yours sit at more than one, the node says so, and the app suggests sending them to one of your own
    addresses first.
- **A locked wallet on Notes, Pools and Bills:** the screen says so and offers Unlock, instead of the node's
  "Please enter the wallet passphrase with walletpassphrase first."

- **A reproducible Linux build.** The Linux program and `.deb` are built in a pinned Docker image
  (`build/linux/Dockerfile`: Ubuntu 22.04 fixed by its digest, Ubuntu's packages from a dated snapshot, Rust 1.98.1
  and Node 20.20.2 checked against their published hashes), on GitHub and by anyone else.
  `build/linux/rebuild.sh v0.2.3` rebuilds them from the tag; the `.deb`'s hash must be the one in `SHA256SUMS`.
  VERIFY.md, step 4, shows how. The AppImage and the Mac app don't rebuild byte for byte yet.
- **Include recent activity in a report.** Report a problem (Settings > Help, or Report this on an error) now
  offers "Include recent activity": the latest lines of the app's own log (it started, the node started and
  stopped, the phone link, failed or held sends, errors shown) and chosen lines of the node's log (its progress,
  start and stop, and errors; never its wallet lines), to the minute, with transaction ids, coin addresses, amounts,
  IP addresses and your name masked. You see exactly what goes before you send it, and it goes only with Send to
  FreeBank, never to GitHub. The phone page's form offers the same, from what the page did
  lately (kept only in its memory).
- **No credit gate.** Notes, Houses, Pools and Bills always show: credit is live from mainnet's start, with no gate
  to open. The app no longer asks the node `getgateinfo`.

## Upgrading from v0.2.2

- **Update the app before the node.** App v0.2.2 can't restart the node with `-reindex`, so a node it updates to
  v0.2.18 stops ("FreeBank stopped (exit status: 1)"). If that has happened, install this app: it finishes the job when it
  starts.

## Security self-audit

What v0.2.2's audit says still holds. What v0.2.3 adds:
- **Who you trust for the Linux package.** Before, GitHub's build machine; now anyone can rebuild the `.deb` from the
  tag in the pinned image and compare. The image still trusts its base image and Ubuntu's packages: a reproducible
  build, not one that builds its compilers from source.
- **One RPC fewer:** `getgateinfo` is off the allowlist.
- **`-reindex` is added only when the node itself stopped saying "Restart with -reindex",** and once per start. It
  rebuilds the node's indexes from the blocks on disk; the wallet isn't reset.
- **The app now keeps a small log of its own,** `<app data>/logs/app.log` (two files of 256 KiB at most, readable by
  your account only). Lines are masked as they are written, amounts included, and sends that went through aren't
  noted (their time could pick them out on the explorer). It leaves the computer only in a report you send with
  "Include recent activity" ticked, after you have seen it. From the node's log a report takes only progress, start
  and stop, and error lines: a payment's lines (amounts, outputs) never go.
- **The window's script policy is unchanged in effect.** Tauri no longer adds hashes of the app's own JS files to
  `script-src` (`dangerousDisableAssetCspModification: ["script-src"]`): with `default-src 'self'` they allowed
  nothing more, and their order depended on the build machine's disk, which kept builds from matching.

## Known limits

- **Macs:** FreeBank v0.2.18's node needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) on Intel Macs and macOS 14 (Sonoma) on Apple Silicon;
  the app picks the right one by itself. Not yet tried on an Intel Mac.
- The AppImage and the Mac app can't be rebuilt byte for byte yet.
- The background part starts when you close FreeBank, not when you log in.
- One wallet per app.
