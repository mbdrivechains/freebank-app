# FreeBank app v0.4.3

A tidy-up of the published source. The app works exactly as v0.4.2 does.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Cleaner source.** Comments, test data, the build workflows and an old release note no longer name people or point
  to private notes and folders. The release key's published home stays where it was.
- Two comments now give the right version for the features they describe (feedback and QR codes, v0.2.1).

## Honest limits

- Nothing new to try: the app's behaviour, its screens and its checks are unchanged.
