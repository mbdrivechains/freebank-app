# FreeBank app v0.4.2 "Names"

Clear coin names, and an app that can keep itself up to date if you let it.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Coin names: ECX and sECX.** ECX is the eCash chain's coin. On FreeBank, or any sidechain, it is **sECX**: one ECX
  brought across through the peg, worth one ECX. The app now says so everywhere:
  - the eCash tab, deposits in and withdrawals out count the eCash chain's coin in **ECX** (it used to say "eCash");
  - Home, Credit, Send, receipts and your phone's approvals count FreeBank's coin in **sECX** (it used to say "ECX").

  The eCash chain, its wallets and the eCash tab keep their names. Nothing about the coins themselves changed.
- **Update FreeBank by itself** (Settings › App updates), **off unless you turn it on.** When on, FreeBank fetches a
  new release in the background (also while its window is closed, if it keeps your phone connected), checks it exactly
  as "Update and restart" does (the release key's signature, then the download against its signed checksum, and on a
  Mac the new app itself), and puts it in place. It runs the next time you open FreeBank; the app says so, with
  **Restart now**. A version that fails to install isn't fetched again by itself, and Settings says why.

## Honest limits

- Automatic updates work where the app can replace itself: the Mac app in Applications and the AppImage. The Debian
  package is updated by Software Updater, as before.
- With automatic updates on, the release key's signature is the only check before a new version is put in place;
  with them off, you see each release first.
- The phone page still says ECX for FreeBank amounts until its next update.
