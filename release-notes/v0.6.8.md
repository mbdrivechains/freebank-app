# FreeBank app v0.6.8 "Know your addresses"

Checks you can run on your wallet, a list of the addresses you gave out, payment exports for both wallets, a steadier
start after a power cut, and two things retired: the old phone relay, and beta.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Is this address mine?** (Settings › Wallet): paste an address and FreeBank says whether this wallet's recovery
  words make it. A FreeBank address is asked of your node's wallets. An eCash address (bc1…, tb1…) is looked for among
  the first 5,000 receiving and change addresses of your eCash wallet and your bidding wallet; your wallet's passphrase
  opens the words for that, and nothing is shown or changed. eCash beta and eCash mainnet share addresses (bc1…) and
  keys, so an address your eCash wallet gave out on beta is yours on mainnet too; easynet's are tb1….
- **Check my words** (Settings › Wallet): type in the 24 words you wrote down, and FreeBank says whether they are this
  wallet's. Nothing is restored or changed, and the words leave the screen when you press Check.
- **Addresses you gave out** (Settings › Wallet): each address Receive hands out, on Home, in the eCash tab or to a
  paired phone, is listed with when, where it went, what it has received, and a note of what it is for. Receive offers
  the note right under a new address. Addresses your wallets gave out before this version are listed too, without a
  date.
- **eCash payments as CSV**: the eCash tab's Payments has Export CSV, as Home's history already had. Both files now
  carry your note for each address.
- **After a power cut**: if FreeBank's node stopped uncleanly, its next start waits (up to 10 minutes) for its eCash
  node to catch up, and says so. Before, it could start against an eCash view still behind, mark a good block as bad,
  and then refuse that block from every peer. If it starts before the eCash node has caught up, such a block is checked
  again once it has, and the Node tab says so.
- **The old phone relay is gone**: phones pair over Nostr only (since v0.6.0 the default). A phone paired before v0.6.0
  is marked "old relay" in Settings › Phone and can't connect: remove it and pair it again.
- **Beta has closed** (26 October 2026): the app no longer offers it. If you are still on beta, the app says so and
  offers easynet; your beta wallet stays on this computer.
- Settings › Wallet's note on rebuilding your wallets elsewhere now says plainly that the eCash wallets sit under
  BIP85's XPRV for your words (since v0.2.6), not under the words themselves.

## Honest limits

- The address check searches the first 5,000 addresses of each kind; one further along isn't found.
- The list of addresses given out lives on this computer: it isn't in the wallet backup, and a restore elsewhere starts
  without it (the wallets' own address books still list the addresses, without your notes).
- Not yet run on a Mac.
