On easynet, a house's notes now count in grams of gold. FreeBank-easynet has started a new chain on FreeBank v1.1.0,
and this version takes you onto it: notes and bills are shown and entered in grams, paid out in sECX at the chain's
payout price for gold, and you can swap gold tokens in their pools.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **easynet's new chain.** The Node tab offers **Update node to v1.1.0**, and the new node starts by itself. The old
  chain's data moves aside, and nothing is deleted. Your wallets keep their keys and addresses. Coins on the old chain
  stay there and aren't listed; get new ones from **Receive › Get easynet coins** or with a deposit.
- **Grams.** Notes and bills show and take grams. Each house card shows the house's own figures: its
  owners' capital and till, the gold tokens it holds, and roughly what a note pays out in sECX today. Notes
  are paid out at par while a house is open; **Redeem** and **Demand** show the spread a stressed or suspended house
  takes while its till is under 2% of its notes.
- **Gold tokens.** **My gold tokens** lists the ones you hold, and **Send** moves one to another FreeBank address.
- **Token pools.** Swap a gold token for sECX and back, or add to its pool. Each trade carries a 1% limit: if the
  pool's price moves further before your trade is in a block, nothing happens, and you can try again.
- **Approve sends on my phone** counts payments in grams at what they're worth in sECX.
- **Plainer errors** when coins aren't confirmed yet, or when a withdrawal's fee is too small.
- **The notice.** On its first start FreeBank shows its notice once and asks for **I understand**; the answer stays
  on your computer. The full notice is in **Settings › Help › About and legal**, and a short line shows where it
  matters: making a wallet, depositing, the house tools, bills and pools.

## Honest limits

- House note pools are closed on gram-notes chains, so the Pools tab shows only gold-token pools.
- Running a house in grams (pledging gold, the till) and netting between houses are from the command line for now.
  The money changer stays off on gram-notes chains until its quotes are in grams.
- **Ways to use FreeBank** still gives amounts in sECX; on easynet it says so.
- The phone companion's own screens (its notes and the houses list) still count in sECX, so on easynet check notes on
  the desktop. Approving the desktop's sends on the phone counts grams correctly.
- easynet's faucet gives sECX again once its new FreeBank wallet is funded; ECX works meanwhile.
- Mainnet stays on FreeBank v1.0.2 until a later release.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
