# FreeBank app v0.2.1

Your phone, made better: pair it on the Home Screen, lock it with Face ID, and keep it connected while FreeBank is
closed. Also: copy your recovery words, report a problem from the app, and FreeBank's sun in its QR codes.

## What's new

- **Face ID for your phone.** On the phone page, Settings > Face ID makes a passkey: Face ID, Touch ID, a fingerprint
  or the phone's passcode. From then on your desktop asks for it each time the phone opens FreeBank, and before each
  send if you choose. The desktop does the checking, so someone holding your unlocked phone can't use FreeBank.
  - Changing or turning off Face ID asks for Face ID too, and after 5 minutes without use (30 minutes at most)
    FreeBank asks again.
  - The prompt doesn't show the payment, so the daily limit and the desktop's approval stay as they were.
  - Settings > Phone on the desktop shows which phones have Face ID, and **Remove Face ID** helps a phone that lost
    its passkey.
- **Keep your phone connected when FreeBank is closed.** FreeBank asks once, after your first phone pairs; later it's
  a switch in Settings > Phone. Closing the window then leaves a small background part of FreeBank running the phone
  link, with the node, while the computer is on and awake.
  - Opening FreeBank takes the phone back. **Stop everything and close** stops it all. On a Mac, ⌘Q keeps the phone
    connected too, as the setting says.
  - With phone sends on, your wallet passphrase stays in the background part's memory (never on disk) until you
    open FreeBank again; FreeBank says so when you close it.
  - With the window closed, a send the desktop would have held for you (over the phone's limit, or with the wallet
    locked) is refused at once, and the phone says to open FreeBank.
- **FreeBank on an iPhone's Home Screen.** The Home Screen icon keeps FreeBank's key; Safari may delete it after 7
  days without a visit. An iPhone gives the icon storage of its own, so the icon pairs itself:
  - on the page the pairing QR code opens, tap **Copy link for your Home Screen app**, add the page to the Home
    Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and tap **Paste pairing link** in the icon;
  - or copy the link on the desktop (Settings > Phone), where a Mac's Universal Clipboard can pass it to your iPhone.
  - The icon is FreeBank's sun. On Android, Chrome offers **Install**.
- **Copy your recovery words** (setup and Show recovery words), after a warning. They go on the clipboard as a
  numbered list for Notes or mail and as plain words for a password manager, kept out of clipboard history, and the
  clipboard is cleared after 60 seconds if it still holds them, or when FreeBank quits. Pasting all the words fills
  the three FreeBank asks back, once they're saved somewhere (a paste straight from FreeBank's own copy isn't taken
  as a backup), and Restore takes a numbered list too.
- **Report a problem or suggest something:** Settings > Help, **Report this** on an error, and the Help menu on a
  Mac. **Send to FreeBank** needs no account. **Open on GitHub** fills in an issue for you to check and post. Security
  problems go privately (SECURITY.md in this repository). The phone page has the same form.
- **FreeBank's sun in the QR codes,** in pale gold behind the dark squares. Nothing else about the codes changes.
- **An earlier FreeBank node folder.** When Setup finds a folder FreeBank set up before, it names its last block,
  wallet and block name, and asks: **Use it** or **Start fresh** (moved aside; nothing is deleted).
- **A new icon,** on Apple's icon grid, without the grey outline that made macOS's light edge brighter.

## Fixed

- Setup no longer shows freebankd's per-block "Failed to get latest withdrawal bundle" line, or its "Shutdown: done".
  After a failed start it shows the node's own error.
- The Node tab says "Your node takes no incoming peers" when `freebank.conf` has `listen=0`.
- The Security panel finds FreeBank's wallet backups in your home folder as well as Documents.
- Obliterate describes a node folder FreeBank used but has no record of creating as exactly that.
- Send shows the right Available and Max when coins arrived while FreeBank was open.
- On Linux, a node left running after the AppImage closes no longer keeps that AppImage mounted.
- Small layout fixes: the phone-send passphrase row keeps Cancel on its line, and Show recovery words opens under its
  own row.

## Upgrading from v0.2.0

- Nothing to do. Phones paired with v0.2.0 keep working; Face ID and keeping the phone connected are optional.
- The phone page updates by itself: app.ecxfreebank.com serves it.

## Security self-audit

What v0.2.0's audit says still holds. What v0.2.1 adds:
- **Face ID is checked by the desktop.** The phone's passkey (ES256, P-256) never leaves the phone. The desktop keeps
  its public key and, for each new session of that phone, checks a WebAuthn assertion over a one-time challenge: the
  origin (app.ecxfreebank.com), the relying party, "user present" and "user verified", the signature and the
  signature counter. Until then the phone gets nothing, not even the outcome of a held send. A proved session lapses
  after 5 minutes without use and 30 in all. A passkey counts only once an assertion shows it works; changing,
  replacing or removing one takes a fresh Face ID, or the desktop. Test vectors and an independent check are in the
  distribution repository (`relay/testvectors/webauthn-v1.json`).
- **The background part** is FreeBank itself, started without a window in its own session. If phone sends are on,
  the passphrase goes to it through a pipe and stays in its memory only, never on disk or in its arguments; it makes
  no core dumps, and on Linux other processes can't read its memory. It sends only within each phone's limit, and
  pairs nothing. When FreeBank opens, it stops the background part (only a process whose command line is FreeBank's
  own for this folder) and waits for it; if the two ever meet at the relay, the background part leaves.
- **Recovery words on the clipboard.** Only when you ask, after a warning; marked for clipboard managers to leave
  out of their history (the Mac and Linux conventions); cleared after 60 seconds if still there. The page itself gets
  no clipboard access: the app writes it.
- **Reports** go over HTTPS to app.ecxfreebank.com, which keeps each as a file readable by the relay alone: the text,
  the kind, a contact if you give one and, if you leave the box ticked, the app, system and node versions. Your home
  folder's path is shown as "~". No addresses are kept. The server refuses control and invisible characters and
  repeats, caps sizes and rates, and holds at most 1000; the FreeBank team reads them escaped, as data.
- **Links.** The app now also opens GitHub's new-issue and private security-report pages for this repository, and
  nothing else new.

What v0.2.1 doesn't protect:
- **A passcode is as good as Face ID.** Phones fall back to their passcode; someone who knows it passes.
- **Face ID protects only once it's on.** Until then a phone is protected as in v0.2.0: by its lock screen, its daily
  limit and your approval on the desktop. Safari and a Home Screen icon are separate pairings: turn it on in each.
- **The background part** runs as you, like the app. Malware running as you could read its memory, as it could the
  app's.
- **The clipboard.** Other apps can read it during the 60 seconds, and on a Mac, Universal Clipboard can pass it to
  your other Apple devices.
- **A sleeping computer** drops the phone link; the phone shows "Desktop offline".

## Known limits

- **Intel Macs** wait for freebankd's Intel build (planned for freebankd v0.2.18); the app picks it up by itself.
- The background part starts when you close FreeBank, not when you log in.
- Face ID is asked again after each reconnect, for example after the phone has been asleep.
- One wallet per app.
