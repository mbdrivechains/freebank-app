If a house doesn't pay your demand in time, you can now protest it from the note card, which gives the house its last
days to pay or suspend payment before it fails; and a failed house's notes can be claimed. The app can also switch to
eCash's mainnet with its eCash wallet alone: real ECX, through an eCash node you name. FreeBank isn't open on eCash's
mainnet yet, so nothing else works there. And sending from the eCash tab works again on easynet.

## What's new

- **Protest an unpaid demand.** A demand's line now says when the house must pay by (about block N). Once that passes
  unpaid, **Protest the unpaid demand** gives the house 7 days to pay or to suspend payment, or what is left of a cure
  already running; otherwise it fails. The 7 days are given once in any 180 days. After a protest the card shows the
  house's deadline, or that it has failed.

- **Claim on a failed house.** A failed house's notes offer **Claim**: they are burned for their share of what the
  chain can seize, mainly its sECX escrow, each gram at its highest bid of the 90 days before it failed. That may not
  cover them in full, and claims close 2 years after the house failed.

- **eCash's mainnet, the eCash wallet alone.** Settings › Node & connection › Network now offers Mainnet. There the
  app runs only its eCash wallet, with real ECX: receive, send (marked so a payment can't be repeated on Bitcoin), the
  card for Sparrow and Electrum for eCash, and Bring in coins. FreeBank isn't open on eCash's mainnet yet, so no
  FreeBank node runs there and nothing can be deposited into FreeBank. You name the eCash node it uses, with its login;
  nothing is built in. The notice for it shows first. Your wallet there can take the words of your easynet wallet.

- **eCash sends work again on easynet.** Since easynet's node update to 4.1 (app v0.6.13), Send and To bidding in
  the eCash tab stopped with "The eCash node's payment can't be read.": that node (eCash v1.2.0) answers in a newer
  payment format. The app now asks it for the one it reads, and an older node still works as before.

- **Downloads try again.** A node update that GitHub answers with a passing server error is tried a few more times
  before it gives up, and a long error no longer runs out of its box.

## Honest limits

- Walked on eCash's mainnet with a test wallet, through an eCash v1.2.0 node on another computer: setting the wallet
  up, receiving, a send to itself (0.0005 ECX, with the mark), the card for Sparrow and Electrum, and Bring in's
  warning. Bring in itself hasn't moved coins on mainnet yet.
- The eCash-only mode needs an eCash mainnet node you can reach: on this computer, or on another with its login and
  that node letting yours in.
- Gold-token swaps aren't in the app yet.
- This version hasn't run on a Mac yet; v0.7.4 has.
