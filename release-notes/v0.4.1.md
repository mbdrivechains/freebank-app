# FreeBank app v0.4.1 "Netting"

Settle with other houses the Scottish way: each house hands in the others' notes, and only the difference is paid.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

**Your node must be v0.2.22 or later** (netting is a hard fork on the beta): the app offers the node's update itself
(Update, where it shows the node's state).

## What's new

- **Settle with other houses: netting** (Credit › Houses › House tools). The Edinburgh exchange: every house in a
  round hands in the other houses' notes it holds, they're all burned, and only each house's net is paid, in ECX at
  par. If A holds B's notes, B holds C's and C holds A's, one round clears all three, where no pair could settle
  alone.
  - **Start a round** with the houses you name, or **paste a round** another house passed you.
  - The app shows the round decoded: each house, what it hands in, its net (pays or receives), and who has funded and
    signed.
  - It offers your house's next step: join, fund (the coins paying your net, and for the house that started the
    round its fee; a house owing nothing still marks its part funded), then sign. The last signature sends it. Then
    pass the round on to the next house.
  - Every house signs, so nothing is settled without you. The app signs only a round at its signing stage with your
    house in it, as shown, and refuses a round that would make your house pay a fee above the 0.001 ECX a round
    started here pays (another house could write one in). Your node checks every bundle against the chain.

## Honest limits

- A round travels as a block of text you pass between houses yourselves (a message, an email). Nothing in the app
  sends it to the other houses.
- Every house in a round must be open, at or above its reserve floor, and have attested its reserves recently and
  since its last settlement (Credit › Houses › House tools › Attest reserves). Starting a round checks every house
  named.
- Funding holds the chosen coins for the round in your node until it's sent or the node restarts: an abandoned round
  leaves them unspendable until then.
- If your notes are spread over several keys, joining first moves them onto one; wait for that to be in a block, then
  join again.
