# FreeBank app v0.4.0 "Tokens"

Run a house's mint: the tokens a phone can hold, backed by your house's notes, with the house's keys kept here.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Tokens: your house's mint** (Credit › Houses › House tools). A house's mint is its own program on its own server:
  it issues Cashu tokens backed by the house's notes, which any Cashu wallet can hold. The house's keys never go to
  that server. From the app the house's partners:
  - **record the mint's keyset** on chain: paste the mint's `keyset.json`, and the app checks it (the house, the
    keyset's id worked out from its keys, the key its records are signed with) before you record it. The mint issues
    nothing until the record is in a block. Paste it again while it waits and the app says so, rather than offering
    to record it twice;
  - **approve a batch lock** the mint made: the notes its float holds, locked on chain as the tokens' backing. The
    app checks the lock with the house's keys and shows what it locks and whose notes it takes; it sends a lock only
    if it takes the mint's float's notes (the float's address comes with `keyset.json` and is remembered).
- **Closing the app** says what stays running: "Close the app, leave the node running", or stop the node too.
- **Receipts** follow a transaction your wallet isn't part of (a batch lock: the mint's wallet pays it) on the chain,
  instead of calling it unknown.

## Honest limits

- The mint itself isn't in this download. On the beta, the first house's mint runs on the beta operator's server; it
  becomes public, and the phone page gets its token wallet, once its address is checked from outside.
- A batch lock is made on the mint's server (one command there) and pasted into the app. Batch burns, and the claim
  if a house fails, come later.
- Tokens are bearer money: whoever holds a token can spend it. The phone wallet keeps the proofs a claim needs; other
  Cashu wallets may not.

## Security self-audit

Agents reviewed the code and ran the app before release.
- **The mint's panel:** nothing that could lose the wallet's coins. Fixed: a batch lock is sent only if it takes the
  mint's float's notes; the partner-signed lock is checked and sent in the app's own code, so it never reaches the
  screens, and the screens can't ask the node to sign one; plain errors when a lock went out, its coins moved, or it
  may have gone; a recorded keyset waits for its block; the keyset's id is checked against freebankd's.
- **A real run of the app** on a virtual display against a local chain, with released freebankd v0.2.21 and a mint:
  the keyset recorded (the mint started issuing at the next block), the record waiting for its block, a lock from the
  float approved and on chain, a lock from another holder refused. Two bugs found and fixed: a lock's receipt said
  "Not in this wallet" with a warning mark, and a line in the keyset's check sat in the wrong column.
