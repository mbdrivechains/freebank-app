# FreeBank app v0.2.7

Small fixes left from v0.2.6's reviews, and what an agent's review of them found.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## Fixed

- **A payment your node took but didn't answer** (a timeout, a dropped connection) now says it may have gone out:
  check History before trying again. It keeps its place in "Approve sends on my phone" and in a phone's daily limit,
  so a payment that did go out can't be counted as if it hadn't. Only your node's own refusal gives the allowance
  back. Trying a send again sends the very same transaction; if it's in the chain already, the app says so. This
  covers Send, Speed up, the Credit tab's payments and a phone's sends.
- **Setup › Advanced:** when Test connection goes green, the checklist above checks again. Addresses you've typed
  but not saved get a **Use these addresses** button, and the checklist says it shows the saved ones until then.
- **Credit › Houses:** a house's long values wrap in their own column instead of under the labels. **House tools**
  folds like the app's other folds.
- **Settings › Wallet:** the wallet card shows what you act on first. Amounts that are zero, the wallet file, the
  transaction count, the key pool and the HD seed id are behind **More details**.

## Security self-audit

- The first fix closes the one gap left by v0.2.6's security re-review: a credit send that timed out after the node
  sent it gave back one payment's allowance. The rule is now one for every paying path: an allowance comes back only
  when the node refused the call itself (`RpcError::did_nothing` in `src-tauri/src/rpc/mod.rs`).
- **An agent reviewed the fix** before release, against freebankd's source. It confirmed that every paying call
  refuses only before the transaction is handed out, and found one Medium and four Lows, all fixed:
  - **Medium:** with "Start when I log in", a phone send whose node stopped answering mid-send read as "starting"
    and gave its allowance back. Now only a call that reached no node reads as starting; one the node may have had
    keeps its count and says it may have gone out.
  - A retry of a send that may have gone out can no longer give back its count, whatever the retry says. Coins found
    spent on such a retry mean it went out.
  - Speed up's answer that the faster transaction went out, with a warning after, counts as gone out. So does an
    answer with no transaction ID.
  - Nothing listening at the node's address (connection refused, or a 403) now counts as "nothing sent", not "may
    have gone out".
  - Setup › Advanced drops a test result for addresses edited while the test ran.
- **A re-review of those fixes** confirmed them, and three smaller points were fixed too: the "may have gone out" mark
  outlives the prepared send's five minutes; only "Missing inputs" reads as coins spent (another refusal shows the
  node's own words); and a Speed up the network refused keeps its count and says it may not go through, rather than
  showing success.
