# FreeBank app v0.2.2

Pairing FreeBank on an iPhone's Home Screen, made plain: scan the code on your desktop from the Home Screen app,
with words on both screens that say which device does what. Also: each phone send listed once, and a guide for
checking FreeBank for yourself.

## What's new

- **Scan the code on your desktop.** The phone page now has a Scan button: it opens the phone's camera and reads the
  pairing QR code your desktop shows (Settings > Phone > **Pair a phone**). It is the easy way in for FreeBank on an
  iPhone's Home Screen, which can't use the Camera app's link. **Paste pairing link** stays, under "Can't scan?".
- **Home Screen first on an iPhone.** The page the Camera app opens now leads with: add FreeBank to your Home Screen,
  open it, and scan the same QR code there. Pairing in Safari is still there, as the second choice.
- **Words that spell it out.** The phone's pair screen lists the three steps and where each happens (on your desktop,
  on this phone, on your desktop again), and the Home Screen app says why it pairs again: iOS keeps its storage apart
  from Safari. The desktop's Pair a phone panel says the same. A Home Screen app's suggested name is "iPhone Home
  Screen", so the desktop's list tells it apart from Safari.
- **Sends from phones lists each send once,** with its latest state. A send that waited for you used to leave its
  "waiting for you" line behind after it was sent. Sends from before v0.2.2 can still show twice, but no longer say
  "waiting for you".
- **VERIFY.md,** for anyone who wants to check FreeBank before trusting it, or have an AI assistant check it: where
  the recovery words and passphrase are handled, every network contact, what the app asks the node, the signature and
  GitHub attestation checks, and what they don't prove.

## Upgrading from v0.2.1

- Nothing to do. Phones paired before keep working.
- The phone page updates by itself: app.ecxfreebank.com serves it.

## Security self-audit

What v0.2.1's audit says still holds. What v0.2.2 adds:
- **The phone's camera.** The phone page asks for the camera only when you tap Scan, reads QR codes from it on the
  phone, and turns it off as soon as it has the pairing code, on Cancel, when you leave the screen, or when the page
  goes to the background. No picture is sent anywhere. The reader, jsQR, is its own file from app.ecxfreebank.com,
  loaded only when you tap Scan, before the phone asks for the camera. The page's
  headers now allow the camera to the page's own site (`camera=(self)`; it was off before).
- **A scanned code is checked like a pasted link.** A link to another site, or to another relay, is refused before
  anything is sent; for any other QR code the page says so and keeps looking. Pairing still needs your Allow on the
  desktop, after you compare the 6-digit code on both screens.
- **What a camera allowed once means.** Your phone's browser may remember that app.ecxfreebank.com may use the
  camera. A changed page served from there could then open it without asking again, so the page you're served is
  checkable: it is a byte-for-byte build of its public source (freebank-phone's README shows how).

## Known limits

- **Intel Macs** wait for freebankd's Intel build (planned for freebankd v0.2.18); the app picks it up by itself.
- The background part starts when you close FreeBank, not when you log in.
- Face ID is asked again after each reconnect, for example after the phone has been asleep.
- One wallet per app.
