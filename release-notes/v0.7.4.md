Each house now pays its notes in sECX at a rate it posts itself: FreeBank sets no gold price. The note and house
cards show that rate, the house's sECX escrow and till, and whether it pays, and a house that fails says so. If you run a house, you post its rate from House tools. This goes with FreeBank v1.2.0, on which easynet has
restarted, so the app follows easynet's new chain. The notice changed with it, so the app shows it once more for you
to agree to.

## What's new

- **easynet's new chain.** easynet's FreeBank restarted on FreeBank v1.2.0 (Gold B). On its first start the app says
  so and offers the node update; it checks the release's signature and checksums, moves the old chain's data aside
  (nothing is deleted) and starts on the new chain. Coins and notes on the old chain stay there. The node updates on
  to v1.2.1 the same way.

- **Each house's own rate.** A house pays its notes in sECX at a rate it posts itself, in sECX a gram: what it pays
  (bid) and what it charges (ask). FreeBank sets no gold price, and the gold price line is gone. Note and house cards
  show the house's rate, its sECX escrow and its till (with the block it was shown at, since it may have spent it
  since), the bills the chain sees as overdue (never the whole story: a house can repay a bill for its borrower), and
  whether it pays. A protested house shows its deadline; a failed one says "Failed: demand unpaid" with its blocks.

- **Demands and redemptions at the rate.** The form shows what you'd get at the house's rate now. A formal demand is
  paid in sECX at no less than the house's rate when you make it; a later cut doesn't reduce it. When you redeem, you
  sign for what you are paid. Paying from your phone counts notes at the house's highest ask of the last 30 days.

- **Running a house: Your house's rate.** House tools has a card to post your house's rate; the first time it also
  registers the key that signs your rates. A house issues notes only with a rate posted in the last 24 hours, and the
  card checks the node's limits before it goes (the ask at most 2% over the bid). The charter form says a house's
  least escrow (0.1 sECX) and that the app charters a solo house; Attest the till replaces Attest reserves, and waits
  while your wallet has a payment not yet in a block.

- **Bills name a reference house.** A new bill names the house whose highest ask of the last 30 days prices its
  repayment in sECX; the form offers the houses with a rate. Each bill shows what it would be paid at.

- **Gone with the chain price:** your house's gold card (pledging gold tokens, the till in gold) and the gold token
  pools. Gold tokens now live on Ethereum, and a house names in its charter the ones it pays out.

- **The notice for Gold B.** The first-run notice and Settings › Help › About and legal carry the new texts, so the app
  shows the notice once more for you to agree to.

- **Electrum for eCash.** The eCash card says stock Electrum can't follow eCash and Electrum for eCash
  (github.com/ecash-com/electrum-ecash) can, on eCash's own network only; its releases carry checksums, not
  signatures. On easynet it says to use Sparrow.

## Honest limits

- Walked end to end on easynet: the update, a house (rate, till, notes), a demand paid by another house, a redemption
  and a bill. The phone's per-house counting is covered by tests, not walked on a phone.
- Gold-token swaps (a demand of 10 g or more paid in gold tokens on Ethereum) aren't in the app yet.
- The app charters solo houses only; partnerships are chartered from the command line.
- A demand or redemption takes whole note coins: to demand part of a coin, move that amount to yourself first.
- House note pools stay closed on gram-notes chains, and netting between houses is from the command line there.
- Mainnet stays on FreeBank v1.0.2 until a later release.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
