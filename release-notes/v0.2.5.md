# FreeBank app v0.2.5

Your house notes on your phone, and your phone as a second check on this computer's payments.

## What's new

- **Notes and houses on your phone.** The phone lists the notes you hold, by house, and every house with its state
  (open, stressed, suspended, failed, wound down) and its reserves against notes out. It sends, redeems and demands
  notes under the same guard as a send:
  - Face ID first, if you chose it for sends;
  - the phone's daily limit, which counts the notes, their 0.001 ECX fee and the carriers (0.00102 ECX in all,
    besides the notes);
  - above the limit, it waits for you to confirm it here.

  Reading notes needs the wallet unlocked for a moment (the node lists them only then). If your wallet has a
  passphrase, turn on "Let my phone send while FreeBank is open" in Settings > Phone.
- **Approve sends on my phone** (Settings > Phone; off unless you turn it on). Once this computer's payments in a day
  come to more than an amount you set, your phone approves the next one with Face ID.
  - **Every payment FreeBank makes here counts, with its fee:** Send and Speed up; the notes, bills, pools and houses
    tabs; and a phone's payment you confirm here, unless that phone's own Face ID already signed it. Approved payments
    don't count.
  - **Your phone also approves what would get round it:**
    - showing the recovery words or the xprv;
    - a new wallet, new recovery words, or a restore from a backup file;
    - Obliterate;
    - pairing another phone;
    - Face ID on another phone (which would let it approve), or removing a phone's Face ID here;
    - a higher daily limit for a phone;
    - turning this off or raising the amount.
  - **Lost your phone?** Your recovery words turn it off, or raise the amount, a day later. Your phones that open
    FreeBank meanwhile are told and can cancel it, and so can you, here.
  - Your phone must have FreeBank open to approve (no notifications yet). You have 2 minutes.
- **⌘Q (Ctrl+Q on Linux) asks what to stop**, as closing the window does, when the node or the phone link would keep
  running. A second ⌘Q within a minute quits. A Mac shutdown or logout never asks.
- **What's running where** (the Node tab): your phones, the relay, this computer (the app, the phone link, the FreeBank
  node), the eCash node and enforcer, and the FreeBank network. Each shows a dot and what happens to it when you quit.
- **Settings > Phone, tidied.** Phones show last seen first, each on one line with Manage. You can remove all phones
  not seen for a week at once. When a phone pairs again under the same name, FreeBank offers to remove the old
  pairing.
- **No grpcurl.** The FreeBank node talks to the enforcer itself since v0.2.17. Setup checks the enforcer with the same
  plain HTTP call, and the app no longer downloads grpcurl.
- **The sync screen** no longer jumps back from "Catching up" to "Warming up", and "Continue while it syncs" no longer
  sits on a blank screen.

## Upgrading from v0.2.4

v0.2.4 offers this update itself: **Update and restart** at the top of the window, or Settings > App updates.
- **AppImage and Mac app:** the update replaces itself.
- **`.deb`:** Software Updater, with the apt repository, or the new `.deb` from the release page.

The phone page at app.ecxfreebank.com is updated too: reload it, or reopen the Home Screen icon.

## Security self-audit

What v0.2.4's audit says still holds. What v0.2.5 adds:
- **The phone's note actions** take the send's guard (Face ID if chosen, the daily limit with fees, held above it,
  refused with the app closed). The phone can't name a payout address for a demand. The desktop checks the address
  with the node before anything is reserved or held.
- **Approve sends on my phone guards the app, not the node.** Someone with this computer and your wallet passphrase
  could still run the node's own commands, copy the wallet, or change FreeBank's files (its settings are a file in
  your account). The setting says so.

  Within the app:
  - every path that pays asks once the day's total would pass the amount, and the plain `sendtoaddress` command the
    screens no longer used is gone;
  - so does every path that would get round it (above);
  - if the phone link fails to start while the setting is on, payments are refused, not let through;
  - each approval has its own random challenge, which the phone's passkey signs, checked here as Face ID always was
    (origin, the signature, the counter, user verification); the first answer wins;
  - an approval goes only to a phone session that has passed Face ID, and only such a session can decline one;
  - the recovery words are checked against the node wallet's own key, and what they change waits a day.
- **Reading notes** unlocks the wallet for a moment with the phone-send passphrase, as a send does, then locks it again.
- **The enforcer check** uses its own HTTP client: no redirects, at most 64 KiB read.
- **Release workflow:** every action is pinned by commit. One action's own reference to another
  (`upload-pages-artifact`'s `upload-artifact`) is by tag, and can't be pinned from here. The Mac build and the Linux
  image install npm packages without their install scripts (`npm ci --ignore-scripts`), and the page builds the same
  byte for byte.
- **An independent review** of all of v0.2.5, by an agent, found ways around "Approve sends on my phone". This release
  closes those inside the app:
  - payments split under the amount;
  - the recovery words shown and then used at once;
  - the credit tabs;
  - confirming a phone's payment here;
  - a second phone quietly becoming an approver.

  A second review of the fixes found more ways round it, also closed here:
  - raising a phone's daily limit;
  - Obliterate;
  - a new wallet or a restore, whose words would then answer "Lost your phone?";
  - Speed up's fee and house pledges not counted.

  The first review also found these, now fixed:
  - the note fee wasn't counted in the phone's limit;
  - stale approval cards on the phone;
  - approval details reaching a phone that hadn't passed Face ID;
  - the phone's clock;
  - redirects on the enforcer check;
  - wording that claimed too much.

## Known limits

- **Approve sends on my phone** doesn't stop the node's own commands, a copied wallet, changed files, or a changed
  clock (the day's total and the recovery words' day follow this computer's clock). A phone still pays within its own
  daily limit without asking.
- **No houses on eCash beta yet:** notes on the phone were tried on a local test chain with a house, with the FreeBank
  node v0.2.18.
- **Notes on the phone** need the wallet unlocked to read, so with a passphrase they need "Let my phone send" on.
- **Touch ID on Macs** waits for the Mac app to be signed with a Developer ID.
- **The first Mac self-update** is this one, from v0.2.4. The AppImage's was tried end to end.
- **Macs:** the FreeBank node needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) on Intel Macs and macOS 14 (Sonoma) on Apple Silicon. Not yet
  tried on an Intel Mac.
- The AppImage and the Mac app can't be rebuilt byte for byte yet.
- One wallet per app.
