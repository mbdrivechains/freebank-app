# FreeBank app v0.6.9 "easynet starts again"

easynet has started again from its first block (reset 4, 29 October 2026), on eCash's new enforcer. This version
brings its new node, and moves FreeBank's easynet node onto the new chain with your wallets.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **easynet-4:** easynet's new node and enforcer. The enforcer is LayerTwo Labs' current one, the same rules as eCash
  mainnet's (one sidechain proposal and one withdrawal bundle a block at most). On easynet the app opens on its setup
  screen, which says easynet has started again; press **Update to easynet-4**. The new node syncs the new chain from
  its first block in a folder of its own; the old chain's blocks stay in theirs.
- **Your wallets come along:** when FreeBank's node next starts, its old easynet chain moves aside to a folder beside
  it (nothing is deleted), and it syncs the new chain. Each wallet keeps its keys, passphrase and addresses; its
  payments on the old chain count for nothing now and the lists leave them out, as do withdrawals made there. The Node
  tab says so. A node an older version left running is started again for this. easynet's balances all start from
  zero: the faucet gives coins again once the new chain's first coins can be spent (a few hours after the restart).
- **The withdrawal wait on mainnet** is now at least 6 weeks (6,048 miners' approvals within 12,096 blocks), since
  LayerTwo Labs' enforcer took eCash's own thresholds; the warning said 3 to 6 months. Its lines about the beta are
  gone.

## Honest limits

- easynet's houses, notes, bills and tokens from before the restart are gone with the old chain; the demo houses come
  back on the new one.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
