# FreeBank app v0.6.1 "easynet coins, and a watch on the eCash node"

On easynet, Receive now gets you test coins from the easynet faucet, and payments link to FreeBank's own easynet
explorer. On any network where FreeBank runs its own eCash node, it now notices when that node or its enforcer stops by
itself, says why, and starts it again.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Get easynet coins.** On easynet, Home › Receive has **Get easynet coins**, which sends a little sECX to your
  FreeBank address, and the eCash tab's Receive sends ECX to your eCash address. Once a day per address; the coins
  have no value. Only the address goes to the faucet. The faucet is also a web page: https://faucet.ecxfreebank.com
- **FreeBank's easynet explorer.** Payments on easynet link to https://fbeasy.ecxfreebank.com, as beta's link to
  explorer.ecxfreebank.com.
- **A watch on FreeBank's own eCash node.** If the eCash node or the enforcer FreeBank runs for you stops while
  FreeBank meant it to run, FreeBank notices within a minute, starts it again, and says so on Home and in Settings ›
  Node & connection, with what its log said ("It stopped normally", an error, or that it was killed). If it stops
  again within the hour, FreeBank leaves it stopped and offers **Start them**.
- **Reports say more.** "Include recent activity" now carries the eCash node's chosen log lines and the enforcer's
  warnings and errors, masked as the rest is (no wallet lines, addresses or IP addresses).
- **No peers for minutes.** The Node tab says so after five minutes without a peer: new nodes find their first peer
  through FreeBank's seed, which may be down. Before, it looked as if the node had frozen.
- **Start them** shows whenever either the eCash node or the enforcer is stopped, not only when both are.

## Honest limits

- The watch runs while the app is open. With "Keep running" on and the app closed, nothing restarts the eCash node.
- The faucet gives 100 ECX or 0.1 sECX per address a day, 50 payments a day on each side, while its funds last.
- Not yet run on a Mac.
