# FreeBank app v0.3.0 "In and out"

eCash into FreeBank and back out, from the app: at par through the peg, or fast through a money changer.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Home: Send, Receive, Deposit, Withdraw.**
- **Deposit at par** from your eCash wallet (the eCash tab's): FreeBank builds the deposit and signs it on this
  computer, with your wallet passphrase. It's credited after the next eCash block and a FreeBank block. FreeBank keeps
  0.00001 ECX of each deposit for the block that credits it; the screen says so before you confirm. **Max** deposits
  everything the wallet can spend. The deposits in flight show below, until credited. From another eCash wallet
  (BitWindow), the address is below as before.
- **Withdraw at par** to your eCash wallet (a new address) or a pasted eCash address: trustless, but very slow. A
  warning comes first, with Cancel; a withdrawal can be cancelled until it joins a bundle.
- **A money changer** (Settings › Node & connection › Money changer: its address and key). With one set up, Withdraw
  offers **Sell to the changer (fast)** and Deposit offers **Buy from the changer: more ECX**, beside at par. Every
  quote is signed by the changer's key, which the app checks with every field it asked for. You trust the changer
  with one order at a time; an order it hasn't paid an hour after you paid in shows as overdue.
- **Payments** name deposits and cancelled withdrawals' refunds (FreeBank credits both in a block's coinbase), and show
  a send's fee.
- And v0.2.9's: updates through Software Updater if you want them, Remove the app too, time left while syncing.

## Honest limits

- **Withdrawals at par take months on mainnet and will probably never complete on the beta**, as the warning says.
- The changer is someone you trust with one order; on the beta it's Michael's, with a small float.
- Not yet: the trustless swap (the third way in and out), the changer directory, and deposits and withdrawals from a
  phone.

## Security self-audit

Agents reviewed the code and ran the app before release.
- **Deposit and Withdraw at par:** nothing High. Fixed: a deposit's confirm now re-checks FreeBank's treasury and
  every coin after the phone's approval, under one lock to the broadcast; a refused deposit is failed and its count
  given back; a new withdrawal shows as waiting for a block; the treasury's opcode is tied to the network; record files
  that can't be read are never written over; and more (V0.3.0.md).
- **The money changer** (the bot and the app's side): one High, fixed: many quotes at once could get round the
  changer's daily limit; the limits now count what live quotes have promised and are checked again before paying.
  Six Mediums, fixed: quote spam, partial payments, lateness judged by the confirming block, reorg and stall
  breakers, the app now checks a payout in its own wallet before showing it as paid, and the eCash payment to the
  changer is recorded before it goes out. The review found no way for the changer to pay one order twice, or for a
  changer to make the app pay more than agreed or to an address it didn't bind. A re-review of every fix found four
  new problems in the changer, among them an over-payment that could be refunded more than once if its payout kept
  failing to build; all fixed.
- **A real run of the app** on a virtual display against a local chain: deposit and withdraw-then-cancel worked; its
  eight bugs are fixed (the worst: Withdraw showed a wrong balance).
- **End to end on a local chain** with released freebankd v0.2.19: deposits built and signed by the app, credited; a
  withdrawal cancelled and refunded; the changer's sell, buy and a late payment's refund.
