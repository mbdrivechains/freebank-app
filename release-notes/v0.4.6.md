# FreeBank app v0.4.6 "Ask FreeBank"

A helper you can ask about anything in FreeBank, and houses that keep their own attest deadlines.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Ask FreeBank.** The **?** beside the gear opens a question box on any screen, in demo mode and in full mode, and the
  demo's "How the demo works" card has one too. FreeBank's helper is an AI that answers from FreeBank's own notes: it
  takes you through the demo step by step, and explains the app's screens, notes, houses, Demand and the rest. It can't
  do anything for you: no payments, no balances, no wallets. The same helper answers questions typed to FreeBank's
  Telegram bot.
- **Houses keep their deadlines.** A house that misses its attest deadline turns stressed and can't issue new notes. The
  deadline counts blocks, and the beta's pace varies, so it's hard to keep by the clock. When a house your wallet runs
  is within 144 blocks of its deadline, or already stressed, Home says so, with **Attest now**. **Attest automatically
  while FreeBank is open** does it for you, 72 blocks before each deadline.

## Honest limits

- Questions go to FreeBank's server and to Anthropic, which runs the AI. Never type recovery words or anything private:
  FreeBank refuses anything that looks like recovery words before it's sent, and the helper never asks for them. The
  helper answers a limited number of questions an hour, and stops for the day at a set cost, on our side.
- The helper knows what FreeBank's notes tell it, and says so when it doesn't know; it can still be wrong. Check what
  matters on the screen it points you to.
- Automatic attests need your wallet's passphrase, entered once. FreeBank keeps it in memory only, while it is open, and
  forgets it when you turn them off or quit. With FreeBank closed, nothing attests: Home's card reminds you next time.
