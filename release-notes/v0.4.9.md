# FreeBank app v0.4.9 "Repay in notes"

A loan from a house can now be repaid in that house's own notes.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Repay a bill in the lending house's notes.** When a house holds a bill you owe, Credit › Bills offers **Repay**:
  pay some or all of it in that house's own notes, at par, and the rest in sECX. The notes go back to the house that
  issued them, as notes flowed home to their issuer between the Scottish banks. You no longer have to cash notes in
  first. "All in sECX" repays as before.
- **Approve sends on my phone** names the notes when a repayment uses them.

## Honest limits

- It needs FreeBank's node v0.2.27 or later (the beta runs v0.2.29). With an older node the screen offers Retire, in
  sECX, as before.
- Only that house's notes count, and only ones not demanded and in a block. Notes go whole, coin by coin: the screen
  says how much fits, and the receipt says how much went.
- On the beta, loans are made through FreeBank's Telegram bot, which now repays in notes by itself.
