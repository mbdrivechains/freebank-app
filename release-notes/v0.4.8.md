# FreeBank app v0.4.8 "Notes and the changer"

A house's notes can now go to and from eCash in one step, through the money changer.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Sell notes for ECX, or buy them with ECX.** When your money changer deals in a house's notes, Credit › Notes has
  **The money changer**: sell that house's notes for ECX, a little below par, or buy its notes with ECX from your
  eCash wallet. The changer signs every quote, naming the house, and FreeBank checks it before you pay, as it does
  for sECX. The notes the changer buys go back to their house, which pays them at par: that keeps notes flowing home
  to their issuer, as notes did between the Scottish banks.
- **The apt page** (apt.ecxfreebank.com) now starts with the download, and with the app setting up updates for you.

## Honest limits

- It needs a money changer set up (Settings › Node & connection) that deals in notes. None runs on the beta yet.
- The changer deals only in houses that are open, not stressed, with no protest and attested on time; it stops on
  its own when a house isn't.
- Selling takes notes from one of your addresses: if a house's notes sit at several, gather them first (Send, with
  the address left empty).
- Buying notes pays FreeBank's transfer fee (0.001 sECX, in notes), so the smallest purchase is 0.01 ECX.
- You trust the changer with one order at a time, as with sECX.
- Not in demo mode: it has no eCash wallet.
