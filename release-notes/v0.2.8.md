# FreeBank app v0.2.8

"Join my bank" in a shop: pay someone who has only a phone in your house's notes, on the spot.

## Download

From the [releases page](https://github.com/mbdrivechains/freebank-app/releases/latest): the `.deb` for Ubuntu and
Debian, the `.dmg` for a Mac, the `.AppImage` for other Linux. v0.2.4 and later offer this update themselves.

## What's new

- **Invite to my house** (your phone, with Face ID): for a members-only house your wallet runs. Your phone shows a QR
  code. The person you're paying (a shopkeeper, say) scans it with their phone's camera, and your phone shows "Allow
  ‹name›?" with a code their phone shows too. Allowed with your Face ID, this computer makes them a wallet of their
  own, with 24 recovery words their phone shows once, and adds its address to your house. A block later you can pay
  them in your notes.
- **Their phone, while this computer keeps their wallet:** their notes and ECX, Receive, payments with Face ID each
  time, and a **Shop** screen: type an amount and a QR code appears; "Paid ✓" when your house's notes arrive; the
  day's takings; a fixed counter QR (any NFC app can write its link to a sticker). Their phone reaches only their
  own wallet, never yours. Your house gives each new wallet 0.01 ECX for the fees that sending notes needs.
- **Scan to pay** (your phone): scan a Shop QR code, or an address, and pay in notes.
- **Moving home:** when they set FreeBank up on a computer of their own, with new words of its own, their phone's
  **Move to my own computer** pairs with it and asks your house to move their money there. Your computer adds their
  new address to the house, sends their notes (redeeming any of another house their computer can't hold) and then
  their ECX, each confirmed before the next. Their old address then comes off your house, money that reaches it later
  is sent on, and the copy here ends up empty: you're asked to delete it.
- **Your address at a house:** Receive, on the phone and here, shows your member address for each members-only house
  your wallet belongs to. That house's notes can only be paid to it; a new address wouldn't be a member.
- **Settings › Phone › Wallets kept for others:** who, which house, where each stands, Try again, Remove Face ID,
  and Delete the copy.

## Honest limits

- **Custodial while kept here.** This computer holds their wallet and its passphrase until they move home, so it
  could spend it. Their phone says so when they join. Moving home sends the money to new keys of their own; after
  that, this copy, and any backup of it, holds nothing they keep using.
- This computer must be running (with "Start when I log in", it wakes for their phone) for their phone to work.
- Keeping wallets for other people is custody. On beta it doesn't matter; before mainnet it's a question for advice
  where you are.

## Security self-audit

An agent reviewed the design and the code before release, then the fixes twice. Nothing rated High; the boundary
between your phones and hosted phones held throughout.
- **First review:** one Medium (restoring the shop's words at home would leave this computer with their keys for
  good: fixed by moving home to fresh words) and eight Lows (the keys file's writes and reads, joining's retries and
  big member lists, "copy deleted" when it wasn't, one invite QR blocking others, the move trusting any computer,
  Shop counting unconfirmed money, an interrupted setup failing for good, payment spam). All fixed.
- **Re-review:** two Mediums in the move home (a move that could stick for good, money arriving late at the old
  address) and two Lows (a wrong computer's address, a member added unseen). All fixed: notes redeemed or skipped,
  fee top-ups capped, Stop moving, the old address taken off the house and watched, the new computer confirming the
  address is its own, and the owner told.
- **Final re-review:** one destination per move (a phone could otherwise add address after address to your house),
  and an emptied copy deleted outright only once the old address is off the house. Both fixed.
- **End to end** on a local chain with released freebankd v0.2.19: two computers, two phones in Chromium with real
  passkeys checked by this app: invite, allow, the wallet made, words, joining, paid by Scan to pay, paid back, moved
  home to fresh words, the copy deleted. Not covered: WebKit and a real iPhone.
